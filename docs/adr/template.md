# NNNN. Title

- **Status** Proposed, Accepted, Superseded by a linked ADR, or Deprecated
- **Date** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders** Names of the people who made the decision

## Context and problem

State the problem, not the solution. Describe what forces a decision now.

## Requirements and constraints

List what any acceptable solution must satisfy. Include limits from the product
plan, security needs, cost, and supported platforms.

## Alternatives considered

Describe each option, what it does well, and why it was chosen or rejected.
Label options that are still undecided as proposed.

## Decision

State what the project will do, in the present tense. Keep accepted decisions
apart from open items.

## Consequences and trade-offs

Name what becomes easier, what becomes harder, and which risks the project
accepts.

## Implementation implications

List the effects on code, tests, documentation, and other ADRs.

## Status and history

Record each status change with its date. Never edit an accepted ADR silently.
Record every revision here.
